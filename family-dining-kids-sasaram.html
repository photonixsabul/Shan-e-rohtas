Why a Kids Play Zone Makes Family Dinners Stress Free

Every parent knows the familiar challenge of dining out with young children. While elders are still looking at the menu or waiting for starters, kids get restless sitting in one chair.

A Space Built for Energy and Curiosity

At Shan-E-Rohtas near Burhan Mor in Sasaram, we designed our dedicated Kids Play Zone right alongside the main dining campus. Equipped with spiral slides, twin slides, climbing steps, and open green turf, children can play safely in the fresh air.

More Quality Time for Elders

Because our covered glass corridor connects the dining area directly to the play lawn, parents can keep an easy eye on their kids while enjoying hot North Indian, Chinese, or Tandoori dishes in peace.
