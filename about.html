The Vision Behind Shan-E-Rohtas

Creating a welcoming landmark in Sasaram where great food, celebration spaces, and open greenery come together for every generation.

Our Story

Crafted for the Pride of Rohtas

Families in Sasaram and nearby Rohtas towns often had to choose between a crowded restaurant with no play space for children or a large marriage hall that felt too big for intimate family celebrations.

Shan-E-Rohtas was built at Burhan Mor, near Mahindra Showroom, to bring all three essentials under one roof: a refined multi cuisine family dining hall, a dedicated air conditioned party hall, and a vibrant outdoor kids play zone.

From our warm Moroccan inspired lanterns and glass facade to our landscaped fountain courtyard, every detail is designed to make guests feel at ease from the moment they step in.
